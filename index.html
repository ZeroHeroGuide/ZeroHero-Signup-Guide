ZeroHero Phone Sign-Up & Battery Setup Guide

Everything you need on one call: qualifying, the pitch, signing up, and walking the customer through programming their battery — for brand-new customers and existing GloBird customers switching plans.

Step 0

Before you pitch — qualify the customer

  • Confirm they have solar AND a home battery — ZeroHero only applies to battery customers.
  • Ask their battery brand and model (have the brand list from the Programming section ready).
  • Check whether they're a new customer (switching from another retailer) or an existing GloBird customer (simple plan change — faster, no switch delay).
"Just to check — do you have a home battery installed alongside your solar? And do you know the brand?"
Step 1

The pitch — 3 ways to save, every day

  • Free power, 12pm–3pm — charge the battery and run big appliances for free, up to 50 kWh/day.

  • $1 a day credit, 6pm–9pm — just by running the home off the battery (up to $365/year).

  • Optional 10c/kWh feed-in, 6pm–9pm — sell excess battery power, first 15 kWh/day (up to $547.50/year).

Step 2

Signing them up

  1. Send the quoteEmail "Your Quote from GloBird Energy" — or action their plan change directly if already a GloBird customer.

Step 3 · The important bit

Battery programming instructions

The battery will not charge from the grid on its own — talk the customer through setting a schedule (or "programming") so it actually uses the free window.

What to set, whichever app they use

If using GloBird's app
  1. Join the VPPCan be done during sign-up or later in-app — globirdenergy.com.au/join-vpp

3 modes to explain: Self-consumption (default — battery just powers the home), Schedule (customer sets the charge/sell times themselves), Auto (GloBird charges and, within limits the customer sets, can sell for them).

If using their own battery's app

Point them to our brand guides, or suggest their installer/manufacturer if their brand isn't listed: globirdenergy.com.au/battery-scheduling

In every brand's app, the customer is looking for the same thing: a charge/discharge schedule or timer setting, usually under Settings → Battery → Schedule (or "TOU" / time-of-use). Walk them to add the two time blocks above.

AlphaESS
In the AlphaESS app: Settings → Battery → Working Mode → set a charge period 12–3pm and (optional) a discharge/export period 6–9pm.
Anker
In the Anker app: Home → Schedule → add a charge block 12–3pm; enable backup/export for 6–9pm if supported on their model.
FoxESS
In FoxESS Cloud / app: Settings → Work Mode → Force Charge 12–3pm; set Feed-in priority or export limit for 6–9pm.
Neovolt
In the Neovolt app: Battery Settings → Time of Use → add charge window 12–3pm and export window 6–9pm.
Redback
In the Redback Ouija/app: Schedule tab → set grid charge 12–3pm; set export/discharge priority 6–9pm.
SAJ
In the SAJ eSolar app: Work Mode → Time-based Control → add charge 12–3pm and discharge/export 6–9pm.
Sigenergy
In the sigenCloud app: Energy Strategy → Custom → add a charge period 12–3pm and an export period 6–9pm.
SolaX
In SolaX Cloud: Settings → Work Mode → Manual/Force Time → charge 12–3pm; Feed-in priority 6–9pm.
Solis + Dyness
In the SolisCloud app: Settings → Energy Storage Control → Time-Charging 12–3pm; set export preference 6–9pm.
Sungrow
In the iSolarCloud app: Settings → Battery → Forced charge/discharge → charge 12–3pm; discharge/export 6–9pm.
WHES (eCactus)
In the eCactus app: Energy Management → Schedule → add charge block 12–3pm and export block 6–9pm.

Menu names vary by firmware version — if the customer can't find it, their installer or the brand's own support line is the fastest fix.

Not sure yet? That's fine — they can sign up now and decide later. Nothing needs to be programmed before the plan starts; they just won't see the savings kick in until it is.

Common pitfall to mention: running the battery flat overnight means buying grid power until solar restarts — encourage big loads (washing, dishwasher, EV charging) in the free midday window instead.

Common questions

Handling objections

"What if it's cloudy and my battery doesn't fill up?"

They can still charge from the grid for free in the 12pm–3pm window — solar isn't required to get the free power.

"Is there a lock-in contract?"

No lock-in and no exit fees — free to join, free to leave.

"Will my power be interrupted while switching?"

No — the switch happens in the background and supply is never interrupted. We'll also confirm by message once it's complete.

"Do I have to sell my power back?"

No — the 10c/kWh feed-in is entirely optional. The $1/day and free power apply regardless.

"My brand isn't on the app list — can I still join?"

Yes — they still get all 3 main savings using their own battery app; we'll notify them if their brand becomes supported later.
